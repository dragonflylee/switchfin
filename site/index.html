01

One app, every server

The same interface, whatever you run

Point GMCA at whatever you already have. Only the actions and tabs change — the experience doesn't.

Plex

Sign in with a 4-character code at plex.tv/link, pick your server and Plex Home profile. Watchlist, hubs, universal transcode.

Jellyfin

Add your server URL, then Quick Connect or username & password. Favorites, resume, Next Up, transcoding — no account required.

Emby

Same flow as Jellyfin, same feature set. Your libraries, collections and genres, gamepad-first.

Stremio

Log in to your Stremio account — your addon catalogs and library sync across. Direct links and debrid streams play straight through.

File servers

No media server? Browse and play from WebDAV, FTP, SFTP and HTTP(S) shares — added straight from the UI, with a connection test.

02

Built for the forgotten consoles

The 10-foot devices nobody ships to

The big media-server apps skip the hardware people actually have under the TV or in their bag. GMCA doesn't.

Homebrew

Nintendo Switch

Native NRO on Atmosphère CFW. Install a HOME-menu tile for full-memory video playback. External USB drives supported.

Homebrew

PS Vita

A first-class target, not an afterthought — a real VPK for jailbroken consoles, on VitaDB. Your whole library, in your pocket.

Living room

Raspberry Pi & Linux

Flatpak for x86_64 and arm64 — a Pi wired to the TV with a controller or keyboard becomes a full media center.

Desktop

Windows · macOS · Linux

The very same app on the desktop — test, browse and play with a keyboard or a plugged-in controller.

Experimental

PS4

A homebrew PKG for jailbroken consoles, for the tinkerers.

03

Built controller-first

Everything your server does

Every feature of your media server, redesigned for a gamepad and a 10-foot screen.

Sign in without a keyboard

Code-based sign-in for Plex and Jellyfin Quick Connect, PIN-protected profiles, account switching — no passwords typed on a console.

Home mirrors your server

Continue Watching first, then every hub your server exposes, in the order it returns them. Your setup, your rules.

Your personal list

Plex Watchlist, Jellyfin/Emby Favorites, the Stremio library — surfaced in the sidebar and on every detail page, whichever your backend uses.

Offline downloads

Take episodes on the road in original quality. One tap grabs a full season — with a storage gauge for your SD card.

Playback with MPV

Direct play and transcode (HLS), resume, chapters, external and embedded subtitles, audio track selection.

H.264 · H.265 · VP9 · AV1 · Opus · FLAC · AC-3 · TrueHD · DTS

Quick actions anywhere

Press X (or long-press) on any poster: go to show, go to season, mark watched, download, add to your list.

Remote file servers

Add WebDAV, FTP, SFTP and HTTP(S) shares straight from the UI — with a connection test. USB drives work too, on Switch.

14 languages

Fully translated interface, rich detail pages with cast and person filmographies, season pages, collections and genres.

05

Up and running fast

Pick your platform

Every release ships builds for all supported platforms.

  1. 1

    Get the build

    Grab the .nro (Switch), .vpk (Vita), Flatpak (Linux/Pi) or desktop package from the latest release.

  2. 2

    Launch it

    On Switch, drop GMCA.nro into sdmc:/switch/ and install the HOME-menu tile for full-memory playback. Elsewhere, just run it.

  3. 3

    Connect & play

    Add a Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or Stremio server — or a file share — and grab the popcorn.
