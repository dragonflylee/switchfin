03 Every feature, designed for a gamepad

Native, down to the metal

No browser, no web view. Compiled straight to each platform, with hardware playback, per-server theming and offline downloads.

Native & fast

Compiled per platform. No Electron, no browser tab. Cold-starts in a blink, even on nine-year-old silicon.

Gamepad navigation

Every screen is laid out for a stick and four buttons. A confirms, B goes back, everywhere.

MPV playback

The mpv engine under the hood: hardware decode, subtitles and the formats that just work.

Downloads & offline

Pull episodes to the device and watch on the train. Watch-state syncs back when you reconnect.

14 languages

Fully translated across fourteen locales. Every string, never machine-guessed.

Per-server theming

The accent follows the source: Plex amber, Jellyfin cyan, Emby green, Stremio violet.

Connection switcher

Keep every server and account side by side. Hop between them from one avatar, no re-login.

Tabs that adapt

The interface changes with the server. Watchlist, favourites and collections show up only where they exist.
